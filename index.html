About

A quick snapshot of who I am and what I care about.

I am a researcher and engineer working at the intersection of generative models, AI security, and critical perspectives on technology. My background is in physics and computer engineering, and my work spans video generation, graph-based models, and watermarking for generative systems.


Beyond technical work, I am involved in queer and feminist organising and translation projects, thinking about how AI can support more equal, livable futures rather than just more efficient markets.

Preprint · 2024 · First author

A mixture-of-experts video diffusion model that handles multiple video editing tasks in a single unified architecture, with improved data curation and distributed training for web video at scale.

Projects

A few things I’ve built or led.

ActiveCMR: 3D Cardiac MRI Reconstruction with Active Acquisition
2024 · Research project

A conditional 3D generative model that reconstructs full cardiac volumes from limited scans, with uncertainty estimation to guide which slices to acquire next.

Medical imaging Active learning Uncertainty
Graph Watermarking for Generative Models
2024 · Research project

Investigating how to embed robust watermarks into generated graphs (such as molecular structures), focusing on the challenges of discrete combinatorial spaces.

Graph generation Molecules Security
